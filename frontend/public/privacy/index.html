Privacy policy

Effective 19 November 2026

The short version
  • No accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking.
  • We don't collect your name, email, location or contacts, and we never sell or share data.
  • Your settings stay on your device.
  • Race alerts are optional. If you turn them on, Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging gives your app installation a token, not linked to you, so the alert can reach it.

Who we are

RI360 (RaceIntel360) is an independent Formula 1 race-analysis app and website (ri360.vercel.app), made by Devarsh. This policy covers the RI360 Android app, the RI360 iPhone app if released, and the website.

What the app stores on your device

These stay on your phone or in your browser. They are never sent to us:

SettingWhy
Light or dark themeTo remember your choice.
Stories you've watchedTo grey out watched stories on the Stories page.
Whether you've seen the introSo the first-launch intro shows only once.
Race alerts on or offTo remember your choice.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes all of them.

Race alerts (optional)

If you turn on Race analysed alerts, the app asks your permission to show notifications and subscribes your device to a single topic called "race-analysed" with Google Firebase Cloud Messaging. To deliver notifications, Firebase creates a registration token for your app installation. This token identifies the installation, not you. RI360 never receives, stores or sees it: the morning after a race we send one message to the topic, and Google delivers it to every subscribed device.

You can turn alerts off at any time with the switch on the Stories page, in your phone's notification settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Race data and hosting

The app comes with race data built in and checks ri360.vercel.app for new races. The website and that data are hosted by Vercel. Like any web server, Vercel may briefly log technical details of requests, such as IP address, browser or app version and time, for security and reliability. We don't use those logs to identify or track anyone.

Fonts and images are part of the app and site, so no requests go to font or advertising networks.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no advertising IDs.
  • No analytics, crash-tracking or behaviour-tracking tools.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of personal data.
  • No access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.

Children

RI360 is a general-audience sports analysis app. It isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date. Material changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
