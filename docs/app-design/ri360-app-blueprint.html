Things to try
  • Overview: swipe the findings carousel (the dots follow) and tap São Paulo.
  • Race: scroll so the big title collapses into the nav bar, switch the Trace / Strategy / Results tabs, and tap Compare for the bottom sheet (drag it down to close).
  • Strategy lab: change a compound or lap count and watch the predicted time count to its new value. Tap Best plans.
  • More: switch Paper / Pit wall. The status bar and every token change together.

Motion system

Animations and transitions

Six durations and three curves cover every motion in the app. Motion follows the same rule as the charts: it explains where something came from or what changed, and never just decorates.

Do it yourself

Figma build guide

Ten steps from an empty file to a prototype you can play on your phone with the Figma app. Budget about two working days if you are new to Figma.

Your plan: RCintel's team is on Figma's free Starter plan with a View seat. Starter files allow only 3 pages, and variable collections have a single mode, so Paper and Pit wall can't be two modes of one collection. This guide works within those limits. A Professional plan removes them and adds 4 modes.
